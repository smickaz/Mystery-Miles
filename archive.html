Elsie's final letter

My dear traveller,

If you have found this letter, you have followed my trail well.

I never buried gold or jewels. What I left behind was something far more valuable.

During my years travelling the outback, I met people who were lost, thirsty, stranded and alone. I wanted a place where every traveller could find water, a warm meal and a helping hand.

So I helped build a travellers' rest stop. The missing mile marker was meant to guide people towards it.

Remember this: the greatest treasure is the kindness we leave behind.

With love,
Elsie Wattle

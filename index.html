An envelope has arrived. Inside is a mystery that has remained unsolved for almost 70 years.

Some secrets are buried in the outback. Others are hidden in plain sight.

One passenger hosts the game and reads the clues aloud. The driver must never operate the phone.

HOW TO PLAY SAFELY

This is a passenger-led game. Never use the phone while driving. There is no need to stop at, approach or touch roadside objects. All clues are fictional and can be solved from the screen.

Play anywhere in Australia. No GPS or special destination required.
